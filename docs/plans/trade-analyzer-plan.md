# Trade Analyzer

**Status:** Implemented · `d7ce6665` · 2026-11-23 · Physical-device and live-price acceptance pending

Trade Analyzer is a third mode in the existing Scan menu. At a card show, scan the card you want, work through the stack you’re offering, and remove the cards the vendor hands back. The camera shows how many cards are on each side and confirms the last card. Review Trade is where you adjust the offer and compare its value.

## Scanner and review

1. Open **Scan → Trade Analyzer**. Keep the existing camera, guide, finish control, and scan options. The mode menu describes Trade Analyzer as “Compare both sides of a trade.” Start on **You receive** so the target card can be scanned first.
2. Tap **You give** and scan one card at a time. The selected side stays active until changed. Use the existing printing, finish, and print-run choices when identification is ambiguous. Confirm the add as soon as the identity is resolved. Price lookup can continue in the background while the camera accepts the next card.
3. Keep the two side selectors and **Review** in one compact row above the guide when space permits. Each selector shows its card count and, once every card on that side is priced, the side’s total; a partial sum is never shown on the camera. The scanner reserves the full card and footer guide at their existing size. Below the guide, a compact Collection-style receipt shows the card name, its price once known, destination side, printed identifier and finish or grade, and **Undo** for the just-added copy. Keep the latest receipt visible until superseded or the copy is undone. Switching scan sides keeps the receipt associated with its actual draft entry. Announce side changes and accepted scans with VoiceOver. The controls reflow with Dynamic Type and yield space to a printing decision; at accessibility sizes each selector collapses to one line (**Give 20**, **Receive 1**) beside an icon-only **Review** so neither side scrolls out of the band above the guide.
4. Open **Review** to pause scanning. The sheet shows the whole trade on one screen: the verdict and both side totals first, then a **You give** section and a **You receive** section, each listing its cards (thumbnail, identity, finish or grade, quantity, price) followed by that side’s cash. Swipe to remove a returned card or switch its side; expand a row to adjust the quantity for verified identical copies, retry a missing price, and see provider and quote age. Undo the last removal from the banner until it is dismissed or review closes. **Done** resumes the camera on the prior scan side; **Clear trade** confirms, then returns to the camera.
5. Keep a cash row in each side’s section. Each side’s total includes its own cash. When both sides have complete values, offer an action that shows the exact amount and side needed to balance the offer. It adds to any cash already on that side. Quantity changes, removals, moves, and cash edits recalculate the comparison immediately. **Clear trade** confirms before replacing a populated draft. Keep one draft in memory while the current scanner model lives so a tab switch, setting change, or brief background interruption doesn’t lose the stack. A relaunch starts a new trade.

## Comparison rules

- Each side totals exact eligible USD card estimates plus any cash on that side. Use the app’s `Money` and price eligibility rules and locale-aware cash input. Difference is **You receive − You give**. Show outcomes such as **You give $5.55 more**, **You receive $3.95 more**, or **Even trade**, under a **Market value** label. A cash-only side is valid. With a complete, unequal offer, **Get $5.55 cash to even it out** (or **Pay …**) closes a $5.55 gap by increasing cash on the receiving side. Keep the amounts editable so the user can reflect the actual offer.
- In review, show counts for quotes still checking or unavailable, and label affected totals **Priced so far**. If a side is empty or any included card lacks an eligible comp, show what’s missing and withhold both the difference and the cash-to-balance action. A still-eligible last-known quote can contribute with an explicit age label and an **Includes last-known prices** note by the comparison. Keep failed and stale states distinct.
- Use the existing exact-product, finish, treatment, print-run, and graded quote rules. An unresolved card prompts review or a retry on the camera. A recognized card with an unavailable quote stays in the draft for review. For raw cards, use the provider’s condition basis when known and expose it with the comp. The camera identifies the card, and the estimates reflect the quoted condition. A market-value difference does not establish a dealer’s margin or account for the work of selling a stack.
- Defer negotiated trade-credit percentages and manual per-card prices until the core flow is tested at a show. A later offer view can show the dealer’s credit and asking price alongside market estimates, with a clear distinction between quoted and manually entered values.

## Acceptance

Local verification used the iPhone SE (3rd generation) on iOS 27.0 and the
`TradingCardScanner-ProfileLocal` scheme. The 156 scanner and camera tests
passed, followed by five focused tests on the final build.

- With one card receiving and 20 cards giving, verify the scanner confirms each card’s name, identifier, finish or grade, and destination without opening review. Switch sides during a delayed identification and verify confirmed cards land on the intended sides. Undo a wrong scan directly from its receipt. In review, remove returned cards, undo one removal, move a card, and adjust a quantity. Check that the counts, receipt, and review totals agree after each action.
- Enter cash on either side, then on both sides. Confirm each amount survives switching the displayed card list and going back to scan. Run cash-to-balance in both directions, including when the lower side already has cash, and verify it reaches exact equality to the cent. Cover cash-only sides, zeroing an amount, invalid input, equal totals, and incomplete quotes.
- Cover same-frame duplicates, deliberate quantities, ambiguous printing or finish, an unsupported quote, a real zero if the provider considers it eligible, stale evidence, missing USD, rate limiting and offline scanning with cached and uncached cards. Verify that each case leaves the draft and comparison in the appropriate state.
- Cover late completions after removal, purpose changes, draft replacement and storage-session changes. Assert Trade Analyzer creates no ownership, inventory, activity or portfolio entries. Run the focused trade tests and existing `ScannerViewModelTests` and Price Check coverage under `TradingCardScanner-ProfileLocal`.
- Render scanner and review states on a small iPhone and iPad, including large Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, and partial prices. On a physical phone, run the one-for-20 workflow under show-like lighting and intermittent connectivity. Record time to comparison, interruptions by cause, and quote coverage. Confirm scanning can continue while quotes load and that changing the offer never requires rescanning retained cards.
