# Scanstash Support

This support page covers Scanstash 1.0 on iPhone and iPad. The app is referred to as CardScanner in its source code and some in-app text.

## Contact

Email [Info@scan-stash.com](mailto:Info@scan-stash.com) for app issues, general feedback, feature requests, accessibility help, or privacy and deletion questions. You do not need an account or a collection export to contact us. For data handling details, see our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

**Repository publication status:** revised support-page draft; not a live website. Complete the [publication checks](README.md) before App Store submission. This status note is for maintainers and should not appear on the published page.

## What to include in a report

Describe whether the issue concerns scanning, card identity or variant selection, pricing, CSV import/export, storage, photos, or a catalog update. Include the app version, OS version, device model, steps to reproduce, expected result, and the error message shown in the app. A redacted diagnostic export is optional; review it before sending.

Do not send passwords, API keys, private account identifiers, certificate IDs, a full collection export, or card images. If a photo or collection sample is needed, we will explain why and ask for your consent. Crop or redact personal information first.

## Scanning and photos

Use even lighting, avoid glare, and keep the whole card visible. Confirm the set, card number, language, and printing when a match is uncertain. If the camera is unavailable, check its permission in iOS Settings. Selected photos are supplied through Apple's photo picker; saving prepared listing photos needs photo-library permission. Scanstash prepares listing photos for you to save or share; it does not automatically publish them to eBay.

## Catalog and pricing

Catalog downloads and fresh pricing require a working network connection. Saved collection records and available offline catalog data remain usable without one. If a provider is unavailable or rate-limited, wait before retrying. For JustTCG issues, check or remove the key in pricing settings without sending it to support. To limit background updates, use iOS Background App Refresh settings; requests while using the app can still occur. Prices are reference information and may be stale or unavailable; they do not guarantee a sale price or a professional grade.

## Collection storage

Scanstash 1.0 stores the collection on the device. iCloud collection sync is not available in this release. Use Settings → Collection & Portfolio → Export CSV to keep a portable copy before changing devices or removing app data. CSV is not a complete backup of artwork, settings, or all historical data.

If the app reports a local storage error, avoid deleting app data and contact Support with the app version, OS version, device model, and coarse error category. Do not send a full collection export unless Support explicitly requests it and you consent.

## Deletion and privacy requests

Delete Collection in Collection & Portfolio settings removes card records and current ownership, but retains removal activity, inventory events, price records, and Value History. To remove local app files, use iOS Delete App rather than Offload App. Remove your JustTCG key separately first because Keychain entries may persist. Delete exported copies and backups separately using their storage service's controls.

For help withdrawing permissions, removing local data, or accessing, correcting, or deleting information you emailed us, contact [Info@scan-stash.com](mailto:Info@scan-stash.com). We cannot remotely access your device-local collection. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for retention and third-party request details.
