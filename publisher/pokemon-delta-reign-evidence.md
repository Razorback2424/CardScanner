# Delta Reign deck and promo printings

English coverage verified November 12, 2026. The retail prerelease decks
contain non-holo Magmortar `DLR 021/103` and Kommo-o `DLR 075/103`. Their
expansion Holo and Reverse choices share the printed numbers. The deck
copies add a Normal choice on each existing card identity. [Deck specimen listings](https://www.pokejoelcollectables.com/product-page/all-2-non-holo-rare-from-delta-reign-prerelease-decks-delta-reign-non-holos)

The first four promos have both a Delta Reign logo printing and a Staff
printing with the Delta Reign logo. Spritzee and Seel are Cosmos Holo.
The Pokémon Center Elite Trainer Box includes a second Kyogre with the
Pokémon Center stamp. [Official product showcase](https://www.pokemon.com/us/news/pokemon-tcg-mega-evolution-delta-reign-product-showcase)

The two deck products belong to group `1840`. The 11 bound promo products
belong to group `24451` and each has an English Holofoil SKU. The November 12
`20:06:19 UTC` TCGCSV build includes 12 quotes across those 13 products. The
stamped Kyogre product arrived with an empty `Number` field list and without a
price row. The app checks its exact product ID, group, name and Holofoil lane,
and accepts a missing number on that product. Its ordinary counterpart
continues to use its own ID. [Shared printing table](pokemon-delta-reign-printings.json)

The `MEP 114` Staff specimen shows the English footer and both stamps. The
full Pokémon capture and direct marketplace checks found only the ordinary
prerelease TCGplayer product for `MEP 114`. The daily catalog comparison can
surface a separate Staff listing when it appears. Keep the existing finish ID
when adding a future binding. [TCGCSV Mega Evolution promo products](https://tcgcsv.com/tcgplayer/3/24451/products)
