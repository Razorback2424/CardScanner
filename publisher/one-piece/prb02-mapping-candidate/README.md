# PRB02 mapping-only candidate

This unsigned revision 4 candidate is based on the signed production revision 3,
independently of the OP18 revision 4 candidate. It adds 265 exact TCGplayer
mappings to existing verified PRB02 printings. No physical identity or existing
mapping changes. Do not sign or publish without a separate owner review.

The retained capture contains the group 24305 product and price responses and
matching upstream build markers. `gap-report.json` records every decision and
the capture hashes. The updated registry, observations, and inventories are the
publisher inputs, produced by `scripts/prepare_one_piece_prb02_market.py` from
the signed revision 3 payload and the retained capture. The publisher verified
the predecessor against the app's production public key and validated this
candidate against that predecessor.

Of the 24 printings without a direct product reference, none was resolved. The
23 standard-art printings have older retailer finish evidence without an exact
marketplace product reference or the retained retailer image comparison needed
to bind a listing to the printing. They are individually listed in the gap
report. OP09-020's full-art alternate is foil-stamped and has no direct retailer
product reference; the marketplace title alone does not establish that stamped
presentation. The captured feed offers competing artwork or treatment listings
for each number, so title/number alone is insufficient to promote any hold.
