# One Piece production candidate 4

This unsigned candidate adds Browse discovery for the 119 distinct English
card numbers listed in [Bandai’s OP-18 inventory](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/cardlist/?series=569118).
[Bandai’s product page](https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/products/op18.html)
dates the release to November 20, 2026. The Browse records retain that
release date.

The base is the [signed production revision 3](../revision-3/release-envelope.json),
retained as the signing workflow's verified predecessor. The
[coverage auditor](../../../../scripts/audit_one_piece_coverage.py) produced
the discovery inputs for series `569118`. The product label and release
date came from Bandai. The standalone One Piece publisher built the
candidate against the retained revision 3 payload.

These observations grant no physical printing, finish, acquisition, or
market authority. Review the post-release inventory and exact market
products separately before attaching any of those facts.
